International Standards

JMK CANARY aligns its inspection and compliance services with internationally recognised standards for occupational health, safety, and road traffic management.

ISO 45001:2018

OH&S Management Systems

The international standard for occupational health and safety management systems. Provides a framework to manage OH&S risks, improve safety performance, and create safer workplaces.

Scope: All workplace OHS audits
ISO 39001:2012

Road Traffic Safety Management

International standard for road traffic safety management systems. Helps organisations reduce road traffic deaths and serious injuries, critical for fleet and transport operations.

Scope: Vehicle roadworthiness inspections
ILO-OSH 2001

ILO Guidelines on OSH

International Labour Organization guidelines for occupational safety and health management systems. A tripartite approach involving government, employers, and workers.

Scope: Workplace safety audits & training
UN GHS Rev.9

Globally Harmonized System

United Nations system for the classification and labelling of chemicals. Ensures hazard communication standards for chemical storage, handling, and SDS documentation.

Scope: Hazardous chemical compliance
ISO 45003:2021

Psychological Health & Safety

Guidelines for managing psychological health and safety at work within an OH&S management system. Addresses workplace stress, burnout, and mental wellbeing.

Scope: Comprehensive OHS assessments
ISO 19011:2018

Guidelines for Auditing

International guidelines for auditing management systems. Provides principles for audit planning, execution, and reporting — the backbone of our audit methodology.

Scope: All inspection & audit procedures
